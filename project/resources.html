Tips for Success

  • Practice coding every day, even if only for 20 minutes.
  • Build small projects to reinforce new concepts.
  • Read documentation regularly.
  • Learn by fixing your own mistakes.
  • Share your projects on GitHub.
  • Stay curious and continue learning.
